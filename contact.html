FAQ

Before you get in touch

How much does a mobile game or app cost?

It depends on scope. A focused prototype usually takes a few weeks, while a launch-ready product takes a few months. After a short call we send a written estimate with a cost range and timeline.

Do you work with startups and first-time founders?

Yes. We help shape the concept, plan a first version and build it with you, with a growth plan for launch.

Do you work with publishers?

Yes. We are open to publishing partnerships and to building games for publishers who need a reliable Unity studio.

Can you help grow a game or app that is already live?

Yes. We can review your store listing, monetization and analytics, then plan ASO, user acquisition and retention improvements.

Which platforms do you build for?

Android and iOS. Our games are built in Unity in 2D or 3D, and our apps are built for both stores.
