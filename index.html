How we work

Prototype, test, then scale

STEP 01

Prototype fast

We get a playable game or clickable app onto real devices in weeks, not months, so ideas are judged on how they feel.

STEP 02

Test with real players

Real people try the build early. We watch where they smile, where they get stuck and where they leave.

STEP 03

Scale what the data supports

Retention, engagement and LTV tell us what to double down on. We invest in what works and cut what does not.
